# EXP-015 — Offensive Line vs Pass Rush Matchup

## Hypothesis

Prior-week pass-protection pressure allowed, matched against opponent pressure generated, may add leakage-safe predictive information beyond v2.2.

This experiment uses only completed prior-week play-by-play. It does **not** use target-game plays, same-week completed games, injury reports, market data, astrology, or Lettrology.

## Data and protocol

- Data source: nflverse play-by-play.
- Discovery season: 2024.
- Untouched confirmation: 2025.
- Frozen observational season: 2026 through Week 3 / 48 completed games.
- 45 preregistered variants.
- Candidate windows: 4 weeks, 8 weeks, full same-season history.
- Candidate pressure modes: sacks, QB-hit-or-sack rate, combined.
- Candidate logit weights: 0, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20.
- Fixed shrinkage: 50 dropbacks toward the contemporaneous league mean.
- Timestamp rule: only plays from weeks strictly before the target game week are used.

PBP coverage in the verified run:

- 2024: **20,215** eligible QB dropbacks
- 2025: **19,830** eligible QB dropbacks
- 2026: **3,521** eligible QB dropbacks

## Locked after 2024 discovery

Brier selection locked:

- window: **full same-season history (`99`)**
- mode: **QB-hit-or-sack rate (`hits`)**
- logit weight: **0.05**

The 2024 discovery advantage was very small. At the locked setting EXP-015 scored **180/272 = 66.18%**, Brier **0.2117**, log loss **0.6124**, versus control **181/272 = 66.54%**, Brier **0.2118**, log loss **0.6122**. The challenger was selected because Brier was fractionally lower under the preregistered selection rule, not because winner accuracy improved.

## Untouched 2025 confirmation

| Model | Accuracy | Brier | Log loss | ECE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| v2.2 control | **180/271 = 66.42%** | 0.2250 | 0.6416 | 0.0840 |
| EXP-015 | **181/271 = 66.79%** | **0.2236** | **0.6385** | **0.0657** |

Delta versus control:

- accuracy: **+0.37 percentage points**
- Brier: **-0.0015**
- log loss: **-0.0031**
- challenger-only correct flips: **4**
- control-only correct flips: **3**
- exact paired p-value: **1.0000**

The locked challenger improved all three primary aggregate metrics, but the winner-accuracy gain is only one net game and is not statistically established.

## Post-confirmation diagnostics

These results are hypothesis-generating only and cannot be used to reselect the 2025-tested specification.

At the locked full-season window and 0.05 weight:

- sacks-only: **182/271 = 67.16%**, Brier **0.2234**, log loss **0.6377**, ECE **0.0920**
- combined sacks + hits: **182/271 = 67.16%**, Brier **0.2234**, log loss **0.6380**, ECE **0.0806**

Neighboring settings:

- weight 0.00: **180/271 = 66.42%**, Brier 0.2250, log loss 0.6416
- weight 0.05: **181/271 = 66.79%**, Brier 0.2236, log loss 0.6385
- weight 0.10: **181/271 = 66.79%**, Brier 0.2229, log loss 0.6374
- 4-week window: **179/271 = 66.05%**, Brier 0.2249, log loss 0.6417
- 8-week window: **179/271 = 66.05%**, Brier 0.2234, log loss 0.6383

The sacks-only and combined variants cannot replace the locked `hits` specification after 2025 was opened.

## Frozen 2026 observation

| Model | Accuracy | Brier | Log loss | ECE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| v2.2 control | **28/48 = 58.33%** | 0.2331 | 0.6602 | **0.1375** |
| EXP-015 | **29/48 = 60.42%** | **0.2314** | **0.6557** | 0.1433 |

Weekly observation:

- Week 1: **62.50% → 62.50%**, Brier delta 0.0000
- Week 2: **56.25% → 50.00%**, Brier delta **+0.0014**
- Week 3: **56.25% → 68.75%**, Brier delta **-0.0065**

The frozen 2026 result is positive overall, but the winner gain is only one net game and the weekly pattern is unstable. Week 3 improved while Week 2 worsened.

## Decision

**KEEP FOR RESEARCH / NO PROMOTION.**

EXP-015 improved untouched 2025 accuracy, Brier, log loss, and ECE versus v2.2, and it also improved 2026 accuracy, Brier, and log loss. However, the 2025 paired winner comparison is only 4–3 with exact p=1.0000, the 2024 discovery gain was extremely small, and 2026 remains a 48-game observational sample. Production v2.2 remains unchanged. Broader walk-forward replication is required before any promotion decision.
