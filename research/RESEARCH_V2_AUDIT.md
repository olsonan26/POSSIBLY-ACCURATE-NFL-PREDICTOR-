## Explicit limitations

- There is insufficient prospective Coach/DeepSeek evidence to estimate incremental utility, calibrate Coach probabilities or promote any new model. Continued genuine pregame captures are required.
- Player-level QB value, charted protection/pressure, historical injury asymmetry and other unsupported historical inputs remain BLOCKED_DATA. Team-stat proxies are labeled as proxies.
- New/renamed/relocated venues without a verified ID/name/coordinate mapping and unknown retractable-roof status fail closed. Historical reanalysis is not an archived pregame forecast.
- Historical feeds may be revised. A reconstruction timestamp never proves that today's bytes were captured before the historical game.
- Promotion remains a separately reviewed action. The dashboard is a saved research-run report and must be regenerated; it is not a live database monitor.
- No production deployment or merge to main was performed by this change.
